Hypno

An AI guide for immersive hypnotic experiences—composing spoken guidance, generated sound, and visual worlds around a conversation with you.

A concept taking shape
Visual fieldConcept study · 01
The experience

A conversation becomes a place to enter.

Hypno explores how an AI hypnotist might shape a guided journey in real time: listening for what you want from the session, then bringing voice, sound, and imagery together into one coherent atmosphere.

01 / CONVERSE

Begin with intention

Describe the kind of experience you want. The guide helps settle on a theme, pace, and style before the journey begins.

02 / COMPOSE

Voice, sound, and scene

Generated narration and sound meet a visual environment designed to deepen focus without demanding attention.

03 / EXPLORE

Stay in your own hands

The experience follows your choices. Pause, redirect, or leave whenever you want; the guide never takes control.

A first interface direction

A world in a phone-sized viewer.

We’re exploring a phone-based VR viewer in the spirit of Google Cardboard: an approachable way to place a generated visual scene around the listener. A regular screen remains part of the idea, too.
